Ejectus — removes macOS junk (.DS_Store, ._ files, .Spotlight-V100, .fseventsd,
.Trashes, ...) from SD cards and external drives.

INSTALL
1. Drag Ejectus into the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications, right-click Ejectus, choose Open, then Open again.
   (macOS asks because this app isn't from the App Store.)
   If there's no Open button: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   scroll down > "Open Anyway" next to Ejectus.
3. A drive icon appears in the menu bar.
4. System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access: add Ejectus and
   switch it on. (Needed to remove .Trashes and .TemporaryItems.)
5. Menu bar icon > "Set Up Spotlight Helper..." and enter your password.
   You'll get a notice "Spotlight helper is on".

USE
- Eject a drive normally: it is cleaned first.
- Or right-click a drive in Finder > Services > Clean Junk Files.
- Or use the menu bar icon: Clean & Eject.
- Menu switches: Clean When Ejecting, Empty Drive Trash (on by default),
  Open at Login. Every clean is logged to ~/Library/Logs/Ejectus.log.
- Skip This Drive (under each drive in the menu): never clean that drive,
  e.g. a Mac-only editing drive.

Only external drives are touched. Never your main disk, Time Machine, or network drives.
Note: deleting ._ files also removes Finder tags/labels on files on that drive.
